# Ethics and limitations

All scenario roles and records are authored fiction. Frozen names may coincide with names in real life; every visual is labeled DEMO and no statement refers to a real person, company, bank or location. Fictional jurisdictions and `demo://` references prevent apparent links to real source pages. Do not use these names to search for or accuse real subjects.

The project excludes private investigative material, real case-specific evidence, confidential bank files, contributor data and calibration inputs. The build performs no external collection. It provides no sanctions, credit, employment or AML decision about any person.

PEP status, offshore location, bank use, family relationship and address sharing are not evidence of misconduct. A red flag prompts collection and assessment, not a legal finding. Synthetic primary records represent a teaching role; they do not assert operational access or formal employment experience.

Source-bounded negatives are crucial: a rejected claim is unsupported in this finite fixture. Unknown current title, beneficiaries, payment purpose and source of funds remain visible. A recorded purchase price is not a current market valuation; a bank counterparty is not necessarily the underlying economic funder.

The validator checks declared metadata, exact references, dates and evidence guardrails. It does not authenticate documents, read arbitrary prose, infer hidden source copying, resolve identities probabilistically, verify foreign law or establish beneficial ownership. A fabricated primary source or mislabeled support basis can pass structural checks; human source review remains essential. The demo is not production-ready software or a universal methodology.

Keep repository private during implementation and validation. The review report is a hostile self-review, not an independent external audit. Public release requires owner approval for visibility and a license decision. Nothing in the scripts or CI changes visibility.
