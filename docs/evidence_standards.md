# Evidence and provenance rules

1. Every substantive record requires explicit source IDs, including rejected propositions and source-bounded unknowns. A search log establishes the bounded collection result, never an underlying negative fact.
2. Every source is synthetic, has a demo reference, date, source type, quality judgment and canonical record key. Quality is a qualitative author assessment, not a weight. Fictional primary bank/registry documents model evidence roles, not actual access.
3. Derivatives declare their parent IDs in `derived_from`. Ancestry can have multiple parents. Cycles and missing parents are errors. S005 derives from S004; distinct labels/publishers cannot make the pair independent.
4. A repeated canonical `record_key` must resolve to the same ancestry. Undeclared duplicate documents are rejected. Unknown copying/shared upstream reporting still requires human investigation: the validator only knows declared lineage.
5. `independent_source_count` is checked against unique documented roots. These are candidate source families, not verified independent witnesses. Corroborated requires at least two roots; multiplicity does not automatically justify a claim state.
6. Confirmed claims in this demo require a primary record, plus human assessment of the exact proposition. A primary search log cannot confirm ownership. Typed checks are necessary but not sufficient evidence review.
7. Probable / analytic judgment requires an explicit basis, limitation, competing explanation and next evidence. CL004's 17-day timing is compatible with partial liquidity, without an earmark. No transaction proves property funding in this dataset.
8. Merger succession and asset-specific title are separate propositions. Historical ownership requires dates. Trust shareholding cannot establish beneficiary identity.
9. Changes to an unresolved state require a versioned reassessment with new evidence and human review. Do not coerce blank/unknown into false or zero. This demo's original states are preserved; it does not implement a production adjudication workflow.
10. Confidence describes judgment about the precise proposition and does not measure criminality, legal certainty or numerical likelihood. Rejected means unsupported by this record, not disproven for all time.

No weighted evidence convergence, MDFI/WARIS calculation, scoring calibration or legal probability is implemented.
